# Brands

<p class="lede">Brand profiles are being researched from current ingredient lists. Each one will cover what the brand avoids and why, its fragrance and preservative choices, its pigments, talc and PFAS policies, and what its certifications actually test, checked against the ingredient pages here.</p>

We won't give brands a single "toxic" score. A brand's marketing tells you its rules; the ingredient list of each product tells you what's in it.
