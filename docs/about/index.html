About and disclaimer

The Lip Ledger cuts through rumors about "toxic" makeup by showing what full safety reports and regulators actually say about each ingredient in popular lipsticks.

For information only

Everything on this site is general information. It is not medical, safety or legal advice, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional.

  • For questions about whether an ingredient is safe, or how much exposure matters, consult a toxicologist, a scientist who studies how chemicals affect the body and at what doses.
  • For an allergic reaction, irritation or any skin condition, see a dermatologist or your doctor.
  • If you think you are having a serious reaction, get medical help right away.

What our ratings are, and are not

  • Ratings describe individual ingredients at typical cosmetic use. They are not a judgment that any product is safe, unsafe, clean or toxic.
  • Ratings are produced automatically by fixed rules from the sources we cite. We may have missed research, and sources can be updated after we read them.
  • "No research found" means we have not found a full safety report yet. It does not mean an ingredient is harmful.
  • Our rules and ratings have not yet been reviewed by an independent toxicologist.

Products and brands

Ingredient lists are copied from each brand's official website on the date shown on the product page. Formulas change, and lists can differ by shade or country, so always check the label on your product. Brand and product names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify products. The Lip Ledger is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any brand, and no brand pays to be listed.
