Top lists

These lists are built automatically from the ingredient ratings and the printed labels. They show how well each formula's ingredients have been researched and what is on the label. They are not safety rankings, and no product is called safe or unsafe.

No brand pays to appear on or be left off a list. Lists cover every shade label we captured for a product. Ingredient lists can change: always check the label on your product.
