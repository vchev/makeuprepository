In lip products:No concerns found

Lip products reach you two ways: through skin contact, and the small amount you swallow. The rating above is the more cautious of the two. Ratings for other ways people are exposed apply to other kinds of products.

On skin and lips

No concerns found

Why: Studies or assessments found no harm.

  • 2026Found no harm

    The US FDA found enough safety data to propose that sunscreens containing zinc oxide at up to 25% are generally recognized as safe and effective (GRASE).

    “FDA's review of publicly available evidence has found sufficient safety data on both zinc oxide and titanium dioxide to support a proposal that sunscreen products containing these ingredients (at concentrations of up to 25%) are GRASE.”

    US FDA, Questions and Answers: FDA's regulatory actions on over-the-counter sunscreen (covers OTC Monograph M020, the 2021 proposed order OTC000008 and the 2026 final orders)

  • Allowed · US FDA

    Listed as safe for use in cosmetics (not limited to "externally applied" cosmetics, which 21 CFR 70.3(v) defines as excluding the lips), including eye area, at good manufacturing practice levels

    US 21 CFR 73.2991 Zinc oxide (color additive listing for cosmetics)

  • 2026Allowed · US FDA

    Permitted sunscreen active ingredient under OTC Monograph M020 (the 2020 deemed final order)

    US FDA, Questions and Answers: FDA's regulatory actions on over-the-counter sunscreen (covers OTC Monograph M020, the 2021 proposed order OTC000008 and the 2026 final orders)
