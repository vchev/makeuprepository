Ingredients/Trihydroxystearin
Ingredient

Trihydroxystearin

In lip products:No concerns found

Lip products reach you two ways: through skin contact, and the small amount you swallow. The rating above is the more cautious of the two. Ratings for other ways people are exposed apply to other kinds of products.

INCI name
TRIHYDROXYSTEARIN

On skin and lips

No concerns found

Why: Studies or assessments found no harm.

  • 2022Found no harm

    The panel's full report concluded this ingredient is safe in cosmetics at current uses and concentrations. The report includes lip-product uses, where small amounts are swallowed.

    “The Expert Panel for Cosmetic Ingredient Safety concluded that the following 51 triglycerides are safe in cosmetics in the present practices of use and concentration described in this safety assessment: Acetic/Linoleic/Palmitic Triglyceride* C12-18 Acid Triglyceride C18-36 Acid Triglyceride C8-12 Acid Triglyceride* Capric/Lauric/Myristic/Oleic Triglyceride* Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride Caprylic/Capric/Lauric Triglyceride Caprylic/Capric/Linoleic Triglyceride Caprylic/Capric/Myristic/Stearic Triglyceride Caprylic/Capric/Palmitic/Stearic Triglyceride* Caprylic/Capric/Stearic Triglyceride C10-40 Isoalkyl Acid Triglyceride Cod Liver/Mink/Tallow Triglyceride* C10-18 Triglycerides Docosahexenoic/ ”

    Cosmetic Ingredient Review (CIR) Expert Panel, final safety assessment, IJT 41(Suppl.3):22-68, 2022 (CIR report 742). The panel is funded by the Personal Care Products Council (industry).

Expert panel reports from the Cosmetic Ingredient Review are funded by the cosmetics industry (the Personal Care Products Council). We use them because they review the published research in full, and we say so every time we cite one.

The Lip Ledger rates ingredients, not products. Ratings are produced automatically by fixed rules from full safety reports and regulations, and have not yet been reviewed by an independent toxicologist. Ingredient lists can change: always check the label on your product. Brand and product names are used only to identify products; The Lip Ledger is not affiliated with any brand.
