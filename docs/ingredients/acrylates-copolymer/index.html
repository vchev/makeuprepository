Ingredients/Acrylates Copolymer
Ingredient

Acrylates Copolymer

In lip products:No concerns found

Lip products reach you two ways: through skin contact, and the small amount you swallow. The rating above is the more cautious of the two. Ratings for other ways people are exposed apply to other kinds of products.

INCI name
ACRYLATES COPOLYMER

On skin and lips

No concerns found

Why: Studies or assessments found no harm.

  • 2018Found no harm

    The panel's full report concluded this ingredient is safe in cosmetics at current uses and concentrations when formulated to be non-irritating. The report includes lip-product uses, where small amounts are swallowed.

    “The CIR Expert Panel concluded that the following 126 acrylates copolymers are safe in cosmetics in the present practices of use and concentration described in the safety assessment when formulated to be non-irritating.”

    Cosmetic Ingredient Review (CIR) Expert Panel, final safety assessment, December 4, 2018 (CIR report 763). The panel is funded by the Personal Care Products Council (industry).

The Lip Ledger rates ingredients, not products.
