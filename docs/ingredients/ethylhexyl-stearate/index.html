Ingredients/Ethylhexyl Stearate
Ingredient

Ethylhexyl Stearate

In lip products:No concerns found

Lip products reach you two ways: through skin contact, and the small amount you swallow. The rating above is the more cautious of the two. Ratings for other ways people are exposed apply to other kinds of products.

INCI name
ETHYLHEXYL STEARATE

On skin and lips

No concerns found

Why: Studies or assessments found no harm.

  • 2015Found no harm

    The panel's full report concluded this ingredient is safe in cosmetics at current uses and concentrations when formulated to be non-irritating. The report includes lip-product uses, where small amounts are swallowed.

    “The CIR Expert Panel concluded that the alkyl esters, listed subsequently, are safe in the present practices of use and concentration described in this safety assessment when formulated to be nonirritating.”

    Cosmetic Ingredient Review (CIR) Expert Panel, final safety assessment, IJT 34(Suppl.2):5-69, 2015 (CIR report 623). The panel is funded by the Personal Care Products Council (industry).
