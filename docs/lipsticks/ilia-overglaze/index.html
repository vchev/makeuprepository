How to read this

Each ingredient is rated on its own for the ways a lip gloss reaches you: on skin and lips and swallowed. The list shows the more cautious of the ratings, and "not yet researched" counts as more cautious than "no concerns found".

A rating is about the ingredient at typical cosmetic use. It is not a verdict on this product.

Ingredient lists can change. Always check the label on your product.

The Lip Ledger rates ingredients, not products. Ratings are produced automatically by fixed rules from full safety reports and regulations, and have not yet been reviewed by an independent toxicologist. Ingredient lists can change: always check the label on your product. Brand and product names are used only to identify products; The Lip Ledger is not affiliated with any brand.
