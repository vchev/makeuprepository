Worth a closer look

AvobenzoneUnclear
  • Skin: Unclear. The US FDA said the public record does not yet hold enough safety data to call sunscreens with this ingredient generally recognized as safe, and asked for more studies, partly because people now use far more sunscreen than when it was first reviewed. The FDA stressed that this is not a finding that the ingredient is unsafe. An FDA clinical trial in 48 adults found this ingredient passes through the skin into the blood above the level at which the FDA asks for extra safety studies. Sunscreen was applied to 75% of the body four times a day, far more than a lip balm. Absorption is not proof of harm, and the authors said people should keep using sunscreen. Allowed by the US FDA: Permitted sunscreen active ingredient under OTC Monograph M020 (the 2020 deemed final order).

How to read this

Each ingredient is rated on its own for the ways a spf lip balm reaches you: on skin and lips and swallowed. The list shows the more cautious of the ratings, and "not yet researched" counts as more cautious than "no concerns found".

A rating is about the ingredient at typical cosmetic use. It is not a verdict on this product.

Ingredient lists can change. Always check the label on your product.

The Lip Ledger rates ingredients, not products. Ratings are produced automatically by fixed rules from full safety reports and regulations, and have not yet been reviewed by an independent toxicologist. Ingredient lists can change: always check the label on your product. Brand and product names are used only to identify products; The Lip Ledger is not affiliated with any brand.
