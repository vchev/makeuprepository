How to read this

Each ingredient is rated on its own for the ways a lipstick reaches you: on skin and lips and swallowed. The list shows the more cautious of the ratings, and "not yet researched" counts as more cautious than "no concerns found".

A rating is about the ingredient at typical cosmetic use. It is not a verdict on this product.

Ingredient lists can change. Always check the label on your product.
