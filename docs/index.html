What the ratings mean

  • Evidence of harm A regulator banned or restricted it for safety, or strong evidence found harm at cosmetic levels.
  • Known allergen A regulator lists it as a cosmetic allergen, or human patch tests show allergy.
  • Unclear The evidence is limited, mixed, or the experts could not decide.
  • No concerns found Full safety reports or a regulator approval found no harm at cosmetic use.
  • No research found We have not found a full safety report yet. A gap in the research is not a sign of harm.

Ratings describe single ingredients for the ways lipstick reaches you (skin contact and the small amount you swallow). They are not a verdict on any product. How we rate

The Lip Ledger rates ingredients, not products. Ratings are produced automatically by fixed rules from full safety reports and regulations, and have not yet been reviewed by an independent toxicologist. Ingredient lists can change: always check the label on your product. Brand and product names are used only to identify products; The Lip Ledger is not affiliated with any brand.
