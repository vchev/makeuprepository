# What's really in your makeup, and does it matter?

<p class="lede">Most "toxic makeup" claims mix up two questions: can an ingredient cause harm under some condition, and does it at the dose and route you actually get from makeup. This site answers both, ingredient by ingredient, with the research and regulations linked.</p>

## Five things to know first

1. **Allergy is the main proven harm.** Fragrance and some preservatives cause most real reactions to makeup. Fragrance-free is the best-supported choice if your skin reacts.
2. **The route changes the answer.** Titanium dioxide's cancer flag is about breathing in fine powder. On lips the open question is swallowing it, which the EU's scientific committee could not settle in 2024.
3. **Some popular swaps don't change your risk.** Black iron oxide instead of carbon black in mascara is a style choice, not a safety upgrade.
4. **The real open questions are narrow.** Asbestos in talc, PFAS in waterproof and long-wear products, and titanium dioxide in lip products are where the science is still unsettled.
5. **Rating apps flag hazards, not risk.** EWG, Yuka and Think Dirty can tell you what to ask about, but their scores don't account for dose. "Clean" labels vary product by product.
